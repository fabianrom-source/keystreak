How XP works

  • 10 XPfor every habit you press, plus 2 XP for each day of that habit's current streak, up to +20.
  • CRIT ×2Every press has a 15% chance to double its XP.
  • JACKPOT ×5and a 3% chance to multiply it by five.
  • +50 XPPerfect-day bonus when every key is down.
  • Level upEach level needs 100 XP more than the one before. Each key you press in a day plays the next note up the scale.
